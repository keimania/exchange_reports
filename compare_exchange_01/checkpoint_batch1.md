## Key Findings (Batch 1)

### Market Model Spectrum
- **KRX**: Pure order-driven, no floor, LP/MM voluntary
- **NYSE**: Hybrid (unique) — electronic + physical floor + exclusive DMM
- **NASDAQ**: Pure electronic, competitive MMs, no floor

### Notable Discoveries
1. **KRX 2025 modernization**: Stop-limit (Feb 5) and midpoint (Feb 27) orders introduced — converging toward US-style order richness
2. **NYSE floor still matters**: D-Orders (floor-only), Auction Collars, DMM capital injection — unique mechanisms not replicable electronically
3. **NASDAQ M-ELO innovation**: AI/ML-adjusted holding periods (Dynamic M-ELO) — first exchange to use machine learning in order type design
4. **NASDAQ 23-hour proposal**: Filed Dec 2025 — would fundamentally change US market structure
5. **KRX dual safeguard layer**: Only exchange with BOTH daily price limits (±30%) AND volatility interruptions
