## Key Findings (Batch 2)

### Market Model Spectrum
- **TSE**: Pure order-driven with Itayose/Zaraba dual mechanism; no general equity MMs
- **LSE**: Unique three-tier system segmenting by liquidity (SETS/SETSqx/SEAQ)
- **HKEX**: Order-driven with structured safeguards (VCM, CAS ±5% band); Stock Connect gateway

### Notable Discoveries
1. **TSE arrowhead 4.0**: Nov 2024 launch introduced CAS and 30-min trading extension — major modernization
2. **TSE Special Quote (Tokubetsu Kehai)**: Unique "slow-motion" volatility mechanism — slows price discovery instead of halting it
3. **LSE three-tier model**: Only major exchange running order-driven, hybrid, and quote-driven segments simultaneously
4. **LSE Named Order**: Last remaining non-anonymous order type on a major exchange (SETSqx)
5. **HKEX Enhanced/Special Limit Orders**: Unique 10-level sweep orders — no equivalent on any other exchange
6. **HKEX VCM**: Trading CONTINUES within ±10% band during cooling-off — not a halt (distinctive approach)
7. **HKEX CAS random close**: 2-minute random closing window with ±5% price band — most structured CAS globally
8. **Stock Connect**: HKEX's unique position as gateway to China A-shares — transforms its role from regional to globally strategic

### Volatility Control Approaches (Batch 2 Spectrum)
- **TSE**: Special Quote (indicative, gradual) + Daily price limits (absolute ¥)
- **LSE**: Dynamic 3%/Static 8% → 5-min call auction (hard but brief)
- **HKEX**: VCM ±10% → 5-min cooling-off within band (trading continues)

### Technology
- LSE Millennium Exchange: 126μs (most transparent latency disclosure)
- TSE arrowhead 4.0: Microsecond-level (newest platform, Nov 2024)
- HKEX OTP: Not disclosed (competitive)
