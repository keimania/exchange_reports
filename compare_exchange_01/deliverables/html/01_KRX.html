KRX (한국거래소) — Exchange Report

1. Exchange Overview

Full Legal Name: Korea Exchange (한국거래소, KRX) Country: Republic of Korea Founding Year: 2005 (formed by merger of Korea Stock Exchange, KOSDAQ Stock Market, and Korea Futures Exchange) [4] Parent Group: Self-regulatory organization under the Financial Services Commission (FSC) [4]

Market Model: Pure order-driven market with price-time priority matching [1]

Asset Classes Traded: [4] [8] - Equities (KOSPI, KOSDAQ, KONEX) - Derivatives (futures and options on indices, single stocks, currencies, interest rates, commodities) - Bonds (government bonds, corporate bonds, REPO) - ETFs, ETNs, ELWs (Equity-Linked Warrants) - Carbon emission rights

Regular Trading Hours (KST, UTC+9): [1] - Regular session: 09:00–15:30 - Pre-market (장전 시간외): 08:00–09:00 (시간외종가매매: 08:30–08:40) - Post-market (장후 시간외): 15:40–18:00 (시간외종가매매: 15:40–16:00; 시간외단일가매매: 16:00–18:00)

Key Statistics: [W] [W] - Listed companies: ~2,600+ (KOSPI ~800+, KOSDAQ ~1,700+, KONEX ~129) - Market capitalization (KOSPI): ~₩3,299 trillion (as of Nov 2025) - Total domestic market capitalization: ~₩3,775 trillion (as of Nov 2025)

Recent Major Changes: [2] - 2025.02.05: Introduction of Stop-Limit Order (스톱지정가호가) — new order type - 2025.02.27: Introduction of Midpoint Order (중간가호가) — new order type - 2026.01.15: Enhanced IT fault handling procedures (전산장애 대응 강화)

2. Institutional Philosophy

Primary Market Model: Pure order-driven (주문주도형), auction-based [1] - All price discovery occurs through the order book via continuous auction (접속매매) and periodic call auctions (단일가매매/동시호가) [1] - No Designated Market Maker (DMM) system for price-setting — unlike NYSE's hybrid model [1]

Design Rationale: [5] - KRX evolved from the Korean Stock Exchange (est. 1956), which adopted an order-driven model influenced by Japanese exchange practices - The pure electronic order-driven approach was reinforced with the launch of EXTURE+ in 2014, emphasizing speed and transparency [8] - All participants submit orders to a centralized, anonymous order book with equal access [1]

Role of Intermediaries: [1] [2] - Liquidity Providers (유동성공급자, LP): Voluntary contractual arrangements for ETFs, ETNs, ELWs, and low-liquidity stocks. LPs provide two-sided quotes but do not set prices [1] - Market Makers (시장조성자): Introduced in 2016 for low-liquidity equities. Obligated to maintain two-sided quotes within contracted spread limits [2] - Neither LPs nor MMs have exclusive rights or informational advantages — they receive fee rebates as compensation [1]

Evolution: Floor-based (1956) → electronic (1997 KOSDAQ) → fully electronic EXTURE (2009) → EXTURE+ (2014) [8] [W]

3. Order Types — Basic

3.1 Limit Order (지정가호가/지정가주문) [1]

  • Specifies security, quantity, and price
  • Executes at specified price or better
  • Applicable: All sessions (regular, pre-market, after-hours)
  • Time-in-force: Day order (default, expires at session end)

3.2 Market Order (시장가호가/시장가주문) [1]

  • Specifies security and quantity; no price specified
  • In call auction: treated as limit at upper/lower price limit [1]
  • In continuous trading: sweeps the book at available prices until filled [1]
  • Applicable: Regular session only (not accepted during pre-market 시간외종가매매) [2]

3.3 Conditional Limit Order (조건부지정가호가) [1]

  • Acts as a limit order during continuous trading
  • Automatically converts to market order for the closing call auction (장종료 동시호가) [1]
  • Designed for investors who want limit execution during the day but participation in closing price discovery
  • Applicable: Regular session continuous trading only

3.4 Stop-Limit Order (스톱지정가호가) [1] — NEW (2025.02.05)

  • Specifies security, quantity, limit price, and stop (trigger) price
  • Activated as a limit order when the last traded price reaches the stop price [2]
  • Sell stop: triggered when last price ≤ stop price
  • Buy stop: triggered when last price ≥ stop price
  • Applicable: Regular session continuous trading

4. Order Types — Advanced

4.1 Best Limit Order (최유리지정가호가) [1] [2]

  • Quantity specified; price automatically set to the best available counterparty price at time of receipt
  • Sell: set to highest buy order price
  • Buy: set to lowest sell order price
  • If no counterparty exists: set to 1 tick beyond best same-side price [2]
  • Functions similarly to a "marketable limit order" on other exchanges

4.2 Best Quote Order (최우선지정가호가) [1] [2]

  • Quantity specified; price automatically set to the best same-side price at time of receipt
  • Sell: set to lowest sell order price (joins the best ask)
  • Buy: set to highest buy order price (joins the best bid)
  • Ensures placement at the top of the same-side book without crossing the spread

4.3 Midpoint Order (중간가호가) [1] — NEW (2025.02.27)

  • Price set to the arithmetic mean of best bid and best ask (중간가격) [2]
  • Fractional won below 1 is truncated
  • Available only during continuous trading session
  • Similar concept to midpoint peg orders on US exchanges, but implemented as a KRX-specific order type

4.4 Time-in-Force Conditions [2]

  • Day (당일 유효): Default; expires at session end
  • IOC / Fill-and-Kill (즉시체결-잔량취소): Executes immediately against available liquidity; unfilled portion cancelled
  • FOK / Fill-or-Kill (전량체결-전량취소): Must fill entirely or is cancelled completely
  • Note: KRX does not offer GTC or GTD for equities — all orders are day orders [1]

5. Order Types — Exchange-Specific

5.1 최유리지정가호가 (Best Limit Order) [1]

  • Unique to KRX in its precise mechanism: auto-prices at counterparty best, not as a market order
  • Differs from a market order because it becomes a limit at the best opposing price — does not sweep multiple levels
  • Rationale: Provides price improvement opportunity while limiting adverse selection

5.2 최우선지정가호가 (Best Quote Order) [1]

  • Unique to KRX: auto-prices at own-side best
  • No equivalent on NYSE/NASDAQ — closest analog is a "pegged to primary" order
  • Rationale: Allows passive participation at best price without specifying a price level

5.3 중간가호가 (Midpoint Order) [2] — NEW (2025.02.27)

  • Introduced February 2025 as KRX's first midpoint execution type
  • Unique implementation: calculated as simple arithmetic mean of BBO, truncated (not rounded)
  • Available during continuous trading only
  • Rationale: Provides price improvement for both sides, reduces spread cost

5.4 조건부지정가호가 (Conditional Limit Order) [1]

  • KRX-specific hybrid: limit during continuous trading → market at closing auction
  • No equivalent on other major exchanges
  • Rationale: Combines intraday discipline with closing price participation

5.5 시간외 Order Types [1]

  • 시간외종가매매 (After-hours at closing price): Trades at the day's closing price
  • 시간외단일가매매 (After-hours single-price auction): 10-minute call auction intervals during 16:00–18:00
  • 시간외대량매매 (After-hours block trade): Negotiated block trades at prices within daily limits
  • 시간외경쟁대량매매 (Pre-market competitive block): Pre-open block at VWAP of subsequent regular session
  • 시간외바스켓매매 (After-hours basket trade): Basket of securities traded as a unit

6. Matching Engine & Price Discovery

Trading System: EXTURE+ [8] [W]

  • Launch: 2014 (replacing EXTURE, which launched 2009) [W]
  • Architecture: Memory-based order book, layered/tiered architecture on x86 Linux servers [W]
  • Throughput: 100,000+ transactions per second (TPS) [W]
  • Latency: 30–50 microseconds average order processing [W]
  • Data Delivery: Solace PubSub+ messaging infrastructure [W]
  • Protocol Support: FIX/FAST for standard connectivity [W]
  • Markets: Single platform covering equities, bonds, derivatives, commodities, carbon [8]
  • Data Centers: Dual — Seoul (equities) and Busan (derivatives) with failover and zero data loss [8]
  • Features: Reference Data Service (RDS) for flexible product additions; Drop Copy and Risk Filter for DMA [W]

Matching Algorithm: Price-Time Priority (가격·시간 우선원칙) [1]

  1. Price priority: Lower sell prices and higher buy prices have priority
  2. Time priority: Among same-price orders, earlier orders have priority
  3. Special rule for call auctions (동시호가): Quantity priority applies — larger orders get priority up to 100× lot size, then ½ remaining, then full remaining [2]

Opening Price Determination [1]

  • Call auction (단일가매매) from 08:00 (order acceptance) to 09:00
  • Orders accumulated during 08:00–09:00 with random closing (+30 seconds)
  • Single price determined to maximize matched volume [1]
  • Market orders treated as upper/lower limit price for matching purposes

Closing Price Determination [1]

  • Call auction during final 10 minutes (15:20–15:30) with random closing (+30 seconds) [2]
  • Conditional limit orders (조건부지정가) convert to market orders for this auction
  • Single price algorithm identical to opening auction

Lot Size [2]

  • Equities: 1 share (1주)
  • ELWs: 10 warrants
  • All other securities: 1 unit

7. Safeguard Mechanisms

7.1 Market-Wide Circuit Breakers (서킷브레이커) [1]

Stage Trigger (KOSPI decline from prior close) Duration Notes
Stage 1 ≥ 8%, sustained 1 minute 20-min halt, then resume Once per day [2]
Stage 2 ≥ 15%, sustained 1 minute (and ≥1% below Stage 1 halt level) 20-min halt, then resume Once per day [2]
Stage 3 ≥ 20%, sustained 1 minute (and ≥1% below Stage 2 halt level) Trading terminated for the day [1]
  • Stages 1 & 2 not triggered after 14:50 (40 min before close); Stage 3 applies anytime [2]
  • Stage 1 timing starts after first minute post-open [2]

7.2 Volatility Interruption (변동성완화장치, VI) [1]

Dynamic VI (동적VI): [2] - Triggered when tentative execution price deviates from last traded price by: - KOSPI 200 constituents: ≥ 3% - Other stocks: ≥ 6% - During closing auction: thresholds reduced to 2% / 4% respectively - Action: Switches to 2-minute call auction [2]

Static VI (정적VI): [2] - Triggered when tentative execution price deviates from last auction-determined price by ≥ 10% - Action: Switches to 2-minute call auction

Exceptions: VI not applied to delisting-pending stocks, overheated stocks, low-liquidity single-price stocks [2]

7.3 Daily Price Limits (가격제한폭) [1]

  • Equities: ±30% from base price (전일종가) [1]
  • Delisting-pending stocks (정리매매): No price limits [1]
  • Special base price calculation for newly listed, re-listed, and capital-reduction stocks [2]

7.4 Trading Halts (매매거래정지) [1]

  • Material disclosure events (공시 관련)
  • Regulatory suspension
  • Technical system failures (전산장애) [1]
  • Resume via 10-minute call auction [2]

7.5 Short-Selling Price Restriction [1] [2]

  • Uptick rule applies to covered short sales (차입공매도)
  • Short sell orders must be at or above the most recent trade price

7.6 Erroneous Trade Relief (대규모착오매매 구제) [1]

  • Large erroneous trades may be remedied upon member application
  • Relief price: ±10% from reference price [2]

8. Special Sessions

8.1 Pre-Market Session (장개시전 시간외시장) [1]

  • Hours: 08:00–09:00
  • 시간외종가매매 (Closing price trade): 08:30–08:40; trades at prior day's closing price
  • 시간외경쟁대량매매 (Competitive block): During pre-market hours; settles at subsequent regular session VWAP [1]
  • Order types: Limit orders only (no market orders) [2]

8.2 Opening Auction (장전 동시호가) [1]

  • Hours: 08:00–09:00 (order accumulation); price determined at 09:00 + random 30 sec
  • Mechanism: Single-price call auction (단일가매매)
  • Order types: All types accepted (limit, market, conditional limit, best limit, best quote)
  • Priority: Simultaneous orders use quantity priority [2]

8.3 Continuous Trading (접속매매) [1]

  • Hours: 09:00–15:20
  • Mechanism: Continuous price-time priority matching (복수가격 개별경쟁매매)
  • All order types available including newly added midpoint and stop-limit orders

8.4 Closing Auction (장종료 동시호가) [1]

  • Hours: 15:20–15:30 + random 30 sec
  • Mechanism: Single-price call auction
  • Conditional limit orders (조건부지정가) automatically convert to market orders [1]

8.5 Post-Market Sessions (장종료후 시간외시장) [1]

  • 시간외종가매매: 15:40–16:00 at day's closing price
  • 시간외단일가매매: 16:00–18:00 via periodic call auctions at 10-min intervals
  • Price range: within ±10% of closing price AND within daily price limits [1]
  • 시간외대량매매: 15:40–18:00 negotiated block trades within daily limits [1]
  • 시간외바스켓매매: 15:40–18:00 basket trades [1]

8.6 Block Trade Facilities [1]

  • 장중경쟁대량매매 (Intraday competitive block): 09:00–15:00; settles at closing VWAP
  • 장중대량매매 (Intraday block): During regular hours; price between day's high and low
  • 장중바스켓매매 (Intraday basket): Basket trade within day's high/low per component
  • Minimum quantity requirements set by 시행세칙 [2]

9. LP / Market Maker Obligations

9.1 Liquidity Provider Program (유동성공급자 제도) [1] [2]

Structure: Contractual arrangement between listed company and member firm [1]

Eligible Securities: [1] - ETFs (상장지수집합투자기구 집합투자증권) - ETNs (상장지수증권) - ELWs (주식워런트증권) - Low-liquidity equities (with issuer contract)

Obligations: [1] [2] - Maintain two-sided quotes (양방향 호가) during regular trading hours [1] - Spread must be within contracted limits (호가스프레드비율) [1] - Minimum quote size: 10× lot size for equities [2] - Exemptions: When trading volume target met, or holding exceeds 2% of listed shares [2]

Privileges: [1] - Fee rebates based on LP performance evaluation - No informational advantages or exclusive order types

9.2 Market Maker Program (시장조성자 제도) [1] [2]

Introduced: 2016 (for low-liquidity equities) [2]

Structure: [1] - KRX selects low-liquidity stocks and assigns market makers via competitive process [2] - Market makers execute via dedicated market-making accounts (시장조성계좌) [2]

Obligations: [2] 1. Maintain two-sided quotes (매도+매수) 2. Spread within contracted obligation spread (의무스프레드) — measured in tick units 3. Minimum quote value (최소호가유지금액) on each side 4. Exemptions: First 3 min after open, during call auctions, when limit up/down reached, when daily traded value > 50% of average [2]

Qualification: [2] - No prior regulatory sanctions within 1 year - Minimum 2 designated market-making staff - Internal controls for short-selling compliance

Performance Evaluation: Quarterly assessment on obligation compliance and active market-making [2]

Compensation: Fee rebates (시장조성대가), differentiated by evaluation results [2]

Termination: Contract ends if stock liquidity exceeds 15% daily turnover for 2 consecutive quarters [2]
