TSE / JPX (Tokyo Stock Exchange) — Exchange Report

1. Exchange Overview

Full Legal Name: Tokyo Stock Exchange, Inc. (株式会社東京証券取引所) Country: Japan Founding Year: 1878 (original); re-established 1949; merged into JPX 2013 [W] Parent Group: Japan Exchange Group, Inc. (JPX) — formed 2013 by merger of TSE and Osaka Securities Exchange (OSE) [W]

Market Model: Pure order-driven market with Itayose (call auction) + Zaraba (continuous) dual mechanism [2]

Asset Classes Traded: [1] [W] - Equities (Prime, Standard, Growth, TOKYO PRO Market) - ETFs, ETNs, REITs, Infrastructure Funds - Derivatives (via OSE — Nikkei 225 futures/options, TOPIX futures, JGB futures) - Bonds (via OSE)

Regular Trading Hours (JST, UTC+9): [1] [W] - Morning session: 09:00–11:30 - Lunch break: 11:30–12:30 - Afternoon session: 12:30–15:25 (Zaraba) - Closing Auction Session (CAS): 15:25–15:30 (NEW — arrowhead 4.0) - Order acceptance: from 08:00 (morning) and 12:05 (afternoon)

Key Statistics: [W] [W] - Listed companies: ~3,945 (Prime: 1,599, Standard: 1,569, Growth: 614, PRO: 163) as of 2025 - Annual trading value (2025): ¥1,419.6 trillion (record high — Prime domestic common stocks) [W] - Market capitalization: ~¥900+ trillion (third largest globally)

Recent Major Changes: [W] [W] - 2024.11.05: arrowhead 4.0 launched — fourth-generation trading system - 2024.11.05: Trading hours extended by 30 min (afternoon close: 15:25→15:30 with CAS) - 2024.11.05: Closing Auction Session (CAS) introduced - 2022.04: Market restructuring — First/Second/Mothers/JASDAQ → Prime/Standard/Growth

2. Institutional Philosophy

Primary Market Model: Pure order-driven, auction-based with dual mechanism [2] - Itayose (板寄せ, Call Auction): Used for opening, closing, and post-halt price determination - Zaraba (ザラバ, Continuous Trading): Used during regular intraday trading - No designated market maker for general equities [1]

Design Rationale: [W] [1] - Japanese exchange tradition emphasizing order-book transparency and fairness - Dual mechanism combines: batch processing for equilibrium prices (Itayose) + continuous matching for real-time price discovery (Zaraba) - No floor brokers or human intermediaries — all electronic via arrowhead [8] - Market maker programs limited to ETFs (incentive-based, not mandatory for stocks) [W]

Evolution: Physical floor (1878) → electronic (1999 system upgrade) → arrowhead 1.0 (2010) → 2.0 (2015) → 3.0 (2019) → 4.0 (2024.11) [W]

3. Order Types — Basic

3.1 Limit Order (指値注文) [2] [3]

  • Specifies price, quantity, and side (buy/sell)
  • Rests on order book if not immediately matchable
  • Time-in-force: Day (default — expires at session end)
  • Available in all sessions

3.2 Market Order (成行注文) [2] [3]

  • No price specified; executes at best available price
  • In Itayose: treated as willing to trade at any price within daily limits [2]
  • In Zaraba: sweeps order book at available prices [2]
  • Available in all sessions

3.3 Market-on-Open (寄付注文) [W]

  • Market order targeting opening Itayose price
  • Executes only at opening price determination

3.4 Market-on-Close (引け注文) [W]

  • Market order targeting closing Itayose/CAS price
  • Executes only at closing price determination

3.5 Limit-on-Open (LOO) [W]

  • Limit order for opening auction only

3.6 Limit-on-Close (LOC) [W]

  • Limit order for closing auction only

4. Order Types — Advanced

4.1 IOC (Immediate-or-Cancel) [3] [W]

  • Executes immediately against available liquidity; unfilled portion cancelled
  • Available for both limit and market orders
  • Used during Zaraba session

4.2 FOK (Fill-or-Kill) [3]

  • Must fill entirely or cancel completely
  • Available during Zaraba session

4.3 Conditional Orders [1]

  • Stop orders converted to market/limit upon trigger price being reached
  • Used for risk management and algorithmic strategies

4.4 Time-in-Force [3]

  • Day: Default — expires at end of trading day
  • IOC: Immediate or cancel
  • FOK: Fill or kill
  • Note: TSE does not offer GTC for equities — all orders are day orders

5. Order Types — Exchange-Specific

5.1 Funari Order (引成注文) [2] [W]

  • TSE-unique: Limit order during Zaraba that automatically converts to market order for closing Itayose/CAS
  • If executed during continuous trading: behaves as standard limit
  • If unexecuted at session close: becomes market order for closing auction [2]
  • Closest analog: KRX's 조건부지정가호가 (Conditional Limit Order)
  • Rationale: Combines intraday limit discipline with guaranteed closing participation

5.2 Conditional Limit at Close [1]

  • Variant of closing participation order with price constraint
  • Participates in closing auction only if price is within specified range

5.3 Special Quote (特別気配, Tokubetsu Kehai) [W] [W]

  • TSE-unique mechanism: Not an order type but a system-generated indicative quote
  • Issued when order imbalance prevents normal price discovery at open or during trading
  • Flags transient order imbalances to attract counterparty orders
  • Moves in increments until sufficient counter-orders arrive
  • Functions as a "slow-motion auction" — unique alternative to hard trading halts
  • Effectively a volatility management tool integrated into price formation

6. Matching Engine & Price Discovery

Trading System: arrowhead 4.0 [8] [9] [W]

  • Launch: November 5, 2024 (fourth generation since 2010) [W]
  • Developer: Fujitsu [W]
  • Infrastructure: 462 Fujitsu PRIMERGY RX2540 M6 x86 servers [W]
  • Software: Fujitsu Software Primesoft Server — ultra-high-speed in-memory data management [W]
  • Latency: Microsecond-level order processing (exact figure not disclosed; prior gen: ~0.5 ms) [W]
  • Reliability: Triple memory replication of trading data; parallel server operation; sub-second failover with zero data loss [W]
  • New features (4.0): Closing Auction Session, 30-min trading hours extension, per-order market information distribution, bulk order cancellation [W]

Matching Algorithm [2]

Itayose (板寄せ, Call Auction): [2] 1. All accumulated orders treated as simultaneous (no time priority) 2. Single price determined to: maximize traded volume, minimize untraded volume 3. All sell orders above the price and all buy orders below execute 4. Used for: opening, closing (CAS), post-halt reopening

Zaraba (ザラバ, Continuous Trading): [2] 1. Price-time priority matching 2. Incoming order matches against resting orders at best available price 3. Execution when lowest ask ≤ highest bid 4. Unmatched portion rests on book

Opening Price (Itayose at 09:00) [2]

  • Orders accumulate from 08:00
  • At 09:00: Itayose determines single opening price
  • If insufficient orders: Special Quote (Tokubetsu Kehai) issued

Closing Price — Closing Auction Session (CAS) [W] [W]

  • 15:25: Zaraba ends; pre-closing order acceptance period begins
  • During pre-closing (15:25–15:30): orders accepted but no trades executed
  • Funari orders and on-close orders registered in order book [W]
  • 15:30: Itayose determines closing price
  • CAS introduced November 2024 to improve transparency of closing price formation [W]

Tick Size Regime [4] [W]

TSE uses tiered tick sizes by price level. For TOPIX 500 constituents (narrower ticks):

For other stocks: wider tick sizes (e.g., ¥1 for <3,000; ¥5 for 3,000–5,000; ¥10 for 5,000–30,000; ¥100 for 30,000–50,000, etc.) [W]

Lot Size (Trading Unit) [1] [W]

  • Standardized at 100 shares for all domestic stocks (since October 2018)
  • Previously varied (1, 10, 50, 100, 500, 1000 shares)

7. Safeguard Mechanisms

7.1 Daily Price Limits (値幅制限) [4] [W]

  • Absolute yen-denominated limits set by base price level (NOT percentage-based like KRX)
  • Base price = previous day's closing price
  • Example: Base price ¥1,000–1,499 → daily limit ±¥300 [W]
  • Limits widen with higher price levels
  • Can be broadened for individual issues experiencing sustained limit-hits [W]

7.2 Special Quote System (特別気配, Tokubetsu Kehai) [W] [W]

  • When opening/intraday order imbalance prevents normal matching:
  • Exchange issues special indicative quote (not a trade)
  • Quote moves in preset increments toward equilibrium
  • Attracts counter-orders gradually
  • Functions as a soft circuit breaker — slows rather than halts price discovery
  • Unique to TSE — other exchanges use hard halts for similar situations

7.3 Circuit Breakers (Derivatives) [W]

  • Nikkei 225 / TOPIX futures: ~8% decline triggers 10-minute halt
  • April 2025: Nikkei 225 futures circuit breaker triggered on tariff concerns [W]
  • Note: Equities on TSE do NOT have market-wide circuit breakers — they use daily price limits + special quotes instead

7.4 Trading Halts [1]

  • Individual stock halts for material information dissemination
  • Resume via Itayose (call auction) mechanism

8. Special Sessions

8.1 Pre-Opening (08:00–09:00) [1]

  • Order acceptance period — no matching
  • All order types accepted
  • Orders accumulate for opening Itayose at 09:00

8.2 Morning Session (09:00–11:30) [1]

  • Opening: Itayose at 09:00
  • 09:00–11:30: Zaraba (continuous trading)
  • Closing: Itayose at 11:30

8.3 Lunch Break (11:30–12:30) [1]

  • No trading
  • Order acceptance resumes at 12:05

8.4 Afternoon Session (12:30–15:25) [1]

  • Opening: Itayose at 12:30
  • 12:30–15:25: Zaraba (continuous trading)

8.5 Closing Auction Session (CAS) — NEW [W] [W]

  • 15:25–15:30: Pre-closing order acceptance (no trading)
  • Funari orders convert to market orders
  • On-close orders registered
  • 15:30: Closing Itayose — single price determination
  • Introduced November 5, 2024 with arrowhead 4.0
  • Rationale: Improve transparency and fairness of closing price discovery

8.6 ToSTNeT (Off-Auction Trading) [W] [W]

  • Hours: Up to 08:20–18:00 (outside regular auction hours)
  • ToSTNeT-1: Single-issue transactions at closing price ± price limit
  • ToSTNeT-2: Basket trading (portfolio-level)
  • ToSTNeT-3: Own-share repurchase system [W]
  • Used for: block trades, basket rebalancing, share buybacks
  • Not anonymous — requires pre-arrangement between parties

8.7 Dark Pool Activity [W]

  • Japan has growing dark pool (PTS: Proprietary Trading System) activity
  • JPX publishes monthly dark pool trading value data
  • As of 2025: PTS/dark pool share growing but TSE lit market remains dominant

9. LP / Market Maker Obligations

9.1 ETF Market Making Incentive Scheme [W]

Structure: [W] - TSE designates market makers for ETF issues based on applications - Incentive-based program (not mandatory for all participants) - Introduced: July 2, 2018

Obligations: [W] - Quote in at least 5 ETF issues - Maintain quotes for ≥ 80% of continuous auction time - Version 2.0 (2026): Display continuous quotes of ~¥100 million - Exempt during trading halts or special quote periods

Incentives: [W] - Fee rebates / reduced trading fees - Quarterly performance evaluation - Tiered incentive structure based on quoting quality

9.2 Equity Market Makers [1]

  • TSE does not have a mandatory market maker program for general equities
  • No DMM-style exclusive assignment (unlike NYSE)
  • No competitive MM quoting obligations (unlike NASDAQ)
  • Liquidity in equities relies entirely on natural order flow and institutional participants
  • Rationale: Japanese market tradition of order-driven purity; ETF MM scheme added only due to specific ETF liquidity concerns

9.3 Comparison with Batch 1 Exchanges

  • vs. KRX: KRX has both LP program (ETF/ELW) AND MM program (low-liquidity stocks); TSE only has ETF MM
  • vs. NYSE: NYSE has exclusive DMM + SLP; TSE has no equivalent
  • vs. NASDAQ: NASDAQ has competitive MMs; TSE has no general equity MMs
