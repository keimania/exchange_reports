- **[The Microstructure Exchange](https://microstructure.exchange/)** - 시장 미시구조 세미나 시리즈 (Optimal Exchange Design, Quote/Trade Races 등)
- **[Six Market Microstructure Research Papers You Must Read (2024)](https://www.globaltrading.net/research-on-the-web-in-2024/)** - 2024년 필독 시장미시구조 논문 6편 큐레이션

- **[Market Macrostructure: Institutions and Asset Prices (NBER, PDF)](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w33434/w33434.pdf)** - 시장 거시구조: 제도와 자산가격 (NBER Working Paper)
- **[Market Microstructure and Exchanges (WFE Chapter)](https://msbfile03.usc.edu/digitalmeasures/lharris/intellcont/Harris%20WFE%20Chapter%20Market%20Microstructure%20and%20Exchanges-1.doc)** - Larry Harris의 시장미시구조와 거래소 챕터 (WFE)

## Key Topics for Cross-Exchange Comparison

### Price Discovery Mechanisms
- Call Auction (Itayose, Opening/Closing Cross) vs Continuous Trading (Zaraba, Regular Session)
- DMM/Market Maker 역할 비교 (NYSE DMM vs LSE Market Maker vs Others)

### Order Types Taxonomy
- Basic: Market, Limit, Stop
- Advanced: Iceberg, OCO, Fill-or-Kill, Good-Till-Cancel
- Dark: Centre Point (ASX), Midpoint (various)

### Volatility Controls
- Circuit Breakers: KRX (8%/15%/20%), LULD (US)
- VCM: HKEX (±10% securities, ±5% derivatives)
- Daily Price Limits: TSE, KRX

### Market Data Protocols
- ITCH: NASDAQ, SGX Titan
- FIX: NYSE Pillar, HKEX OCG, SGX Titan OTC
- Binary: NYSE Pillar, HKEX OCG/OMD, Eurex T7 ETI
